DOC_ID: 19_heat_stress_fruit_set_tomato
TITLE: Heat stress, flower drop and poor fruit set in tomato
CROPS: tomato (chilli/pepper flowers also affected per one source)
SENSOR_SIGNALS: temperature_c day above ~26-30 C and night above ~20-24 C for hours to days; humidity above 80% for long periods

FACTS
- Optimum for growth: 25-30 C by day and about 20 C at night; above about 35 C day and/or 30 C night fruit set falls (pollen viability, fewer flowers) [S1].
- Day/night temperatures above 26/20 C can significantly affect fruit set and yield; day temperature above 25 C reduces fruit number, weight and seeds per fruit [S2]. Pollen germination and tube growth fall above 30 C [S2].
- Ideal daily mean temperature for fruit set under field conditions is 21-24 C [S3].
- Night temperature may matter more than day temperature: ideal fruit set occurs within about 15.5-21 C (60-70 F) at night; above about 24 C (75 F) pollen-tube growth is disturbed and blossom drop results. Prolonged humidity above 80% hinders pollen shedding [S4].
- Sustained heat of more than about 29 C by day and 21 C at night stresses plants; during about 50-hour hot spells a round of flowers may drop unpollinated [S5]. Day temperatures above 32 C with night above 21 C make buds fall rather than set fruit; cooler nights reduce the damage [S6].
- Red pigment does not form above about 29 C (85 F); optimum ripening is 20-25 C (68-77 F) [S7].
- Moving from 28/22 C to 32/26 C day/night reduced fruit set in a controlled study [S8].

MANAGEMENT (as stated by sources)
- Heat-tolerant varieties set fruit under hot nights (above 21 C) [S2][S5]. Fruit-set hormone sprays can give puffy or malformed fruit, and no growth regulator makes normal fruit under high heat [S4][S6]. Keep notes on variety performance [S5].
