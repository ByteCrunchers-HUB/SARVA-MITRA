TITLE: Other ragi diseases, resistant varieties and growing notes
CROPS: ragi / finger millet
SENSOR_SIGNALS: weak; mostly crop-stage and variety information

FACTS
- Finger millet is susceptible to blast, sheath blight, seedling blight, foot rot, smut, leaf spot, green ear, damping-off, streak and mottling viruses [S1].
- ICAR-IIMR: seedling diseases (root rot, damping-off) appear at seedling stage; Cercospora and Helminthosporium leaf spots occur in finger millet and are sporadic [S2].
- A book chapter lists crazy top, smut, downy mildew, foot rot, banded blight, sheath blight, bacterial leaf spot/blight/stripe, and mottle streak, severe mosaic and streak virus diseases; blast is the most destructive [S3].
- Helminthosporium leaf spot: small oval brown lesions on young leaves that join into blighted patches (course slides; secondary source) [S4].
- Varieties (ICAR Oct 2025): CFMV-1 (Indravathi), recommended for Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Odisha, is listed as resistant to finger blast, neck blast, banded blight and foot rot. CFMV-2 is resistant to leaf blast, foot rot, brown spot and grain mold and moderately resistant to neck and finger blast and banded blight [S5].
- Growing notes (ICAR 2025): about 90% of the area is kharif rainfed; ragi prefers loamy to sandy soil with good drainage [S5].
- Regional note: Karnataka has the most ragi area, with significant production in Bengaluru, Kolar, Tumakuru, Chitradurga and Hassan districts [S6].
- Seed treatment and foliar fungicides are mentioned for blast and leaf spot in course slides; follow local label advice [S4].
