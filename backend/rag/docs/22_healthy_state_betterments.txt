TITLE: Practices the sources recommend to keep crops healthy (use when disease = 0)
TYPE: advice compiled only from statements in the retrieved sources. Pick the ones that match the farmer's current readings; do not dump all.
SENSOR_SIGNALS: any; match the tip to the value (humidity, leaf_wet, soil moisture, EC, temperature)

IF HUMIDITY OR LEAF WETNESS IS OFTEN HIGH
- Use drip irrigation instead of overhead; avoid working in wet plants [S1]. Water at the base and avoid wetting foliage [S2]. Water in late morning when soil is dry [S3]. Avoid overhead and night-time irrigation to cut late blight risk [S4].
- Keep plant spacing for air flow; prune the lowest 3-4 leaf branches once plants are established and fruiting [S2][S5]. Do not strip leaves above the fruit, which risks sunscald [S6]. Pruning or trellising can add stress and raise blossom-end rot [S7].
- Scout low, shaded and damp spots first; use weather-based risk models to time checks [S4][S8].

IF SOIL IS WET
- Improve drainage, use raised beds, avoid overwatering [S3]. Manage soil moisture to reduce bacterial wilt [S9]. Do not move wet soil on tools or shoes [S10].

IF SOIL IS DRY OR WATERING IS UNEVEN
- Water deeply on a regular schedule, especially in hot weather; keep soil evenly moist [S11][S12]. Mulch may help [S7]. Plants under stress suffer more early blight [S13].

IF SOIL EC IS RISING
- Keep irrigation water below about 4 dS/m and watch root-zone EC against the tomato threshold of about 2.5 dS/m [S14][S15]. Use drip irrigation when water is salty [S16]. Fertilise moderately to avoid salt build-up [S7].

IF TEMPERATURE IS HIGH
- Sustained heat above about 29 C by day and 21 C at night can cause flower drop; heat-tolerant varieties help [S17]. See file 19.

NUTRITION
- Avoid too much nitrogen: it favours powdery mildew [S18], Fusarium wilt (with low potassium) [S10] and blossom-end rot [S7]. Soil-test calcium and potassium [S7][S10].

FIELD HYGIENE AND PLANTING
- Use disease-free seed and seedlings; remove crop debris; control nightshade-family weeds; rotate crops [S5][S13][S19]. Clean tools, stakes and crates [S10]. Hot-water seed treatment is mentioned for early blight (50 C for 25 minutes) [S19].
- Choose resistant or tolerant varieties recommended locally [S19][S20]. For ragi, early sowing, resistant varieties and rotation with legumes are listed (file 15).
- Whitefly and leaf curl: remove infected plants and weed hosts; yellow sticky traps (file 12).
