TITLE: Tomato leaf curl virus spread by whitefly
CROPS: tomato; chilli leaf curl is also reported
SENSOR_SIGNALS: none reliable. The sources give no sensor thresholds; this disease depends on whitefly presence. Use only when the farmer reports curling leaves.

FACTS
- Symptoms: severe stunting, downward rolling and crinkling of leaves, older leaves leathery and brittle, short internodes, bushy look with more side branches [S1].
- The virus is carried by the whitefly Bemisia tabaci; primary spread is by whiteflies and secondary spread by movement of infected plants [S1].
- Whitefly damage includes chlorotic spots, yellowing and downward curling and drying of leaves [S2].
- Vector activity, virus multiplication and symptom development are affected by the weather during and after infection (research paper, no thresholds given) [S3].

MANAGEMENT (as stated by TNAU)
- Uproot and destroy leaf-curl plants; use nitrogen and irrigation judiciously; remove the weed host Abutilon indicum; use yellow sticky traps (12 per hectare) [S2]. A TNAU advisory for chilli leaf curl also recommends yellow sticky traps (15 per hectare) with insecticide choices; product and dose must come from TNAU/KVK labels [S4].
