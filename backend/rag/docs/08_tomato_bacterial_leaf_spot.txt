DOC_ID: 08_tomato_bacterial_leaf_spot
TITLE: Bacterial leaf spot of tomato - moist weather with splashing rain
CROPS: tomato (the same source also covers chilli bacterial leaf spot)
SENSOR_SIGNALS: rainfall_mm > 0 (splashing rain); humidity high; persistent dew / leaf_wet = 1; moderate temperature

FACTS
- Moist weather and splattering rains favour the disease, with high humidity or persistent dew [S1][S2].
- Bacteria survive on infected plant debris and seed (inside and outside the seed) and on nightshade-family weeds such as Solanum nigrum; secondary spread is by rain splash [S1].
- For chilli bacterial leaf spot the same source lists moderate temperature, high relative humidity and intermittent rains as favourable, with seed-borne primary infection and rain-splash spread [S3].
- COMPILER NOTE: the NIPHM page text is partly scrambled, so only statements clearly tied to "bacterial leaf spot" are repeated here. No temperature threshold was reliably extractable. Ask the team to read the original page before relying on more detail.
