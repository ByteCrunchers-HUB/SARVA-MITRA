TITLE: Other chilli diseases and the conditions that favour them (NIPHM list)
CROPS: chilli
SENSOR_SIGNALS: see each line

FACTS (all from NIPHM/DPPQS chilli IPM page unless noted)
- Damping-off (Pythium, Rhizoctonia): heavy rain, frequent irrigation, poor drainage, close spacing; high soil moisture with 25-30 C (Pythium) or 30-35 C (Rhizoctonia) [S1]. See file 11.
- Powdery mildew (Leveillula taurica): cool dry weather favours spore germination; high humidity favours disease development; white growth on lower leaf surface [S1][S2]. See file 06.
- Cercospora leaf spot and Alternaria leaf spot: warm, rainy and wet weather [S1].
- Fusarium wilt: relatively high soil moisture and soil temperature; spreads by soil and implements [S1].
- Bacterial leaf spot: moderate temperature, high humidity, intermittent rain; seed-borne [S1].
- Chilli mosaic (CMV): spread by seed to some extent and by aphid vectors from collateral hosts [S1].
- Anthracnose/dieback: see file 13.
- A TNAU powdery mildew note for chilli: sprays of wettable sulphur or carbendazim at 15-day intervals from first symptoms (product choice and dose per TNAU/KVK) [S2].
