IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, KA) Major viruses in Karnataka tomato/chilli:
  - ToLCV (Tomato leaf curl virus) - whitefly vector [12]
  - ChiLCV (Chilli leaf curl virus) - whitefly vector [chilli_viral_diseases]
  - CMV (Cucumber mosaic virus) - aphid vector [chilli_viral_diseases]
  - ChiVMV (Chilli veinal mottle virus) - aphid vector [chilli_viral_diseases]
  - TSWV/GBNV (Tomato spotted wilt / Groundnut bud necrosis) - thrips vector [chilli_viral_diseases]
- (OBS, KA) Vector details in pest_whitefly.txt, pest_aphids_thrips.txt.

VECTOR CONFIRMATION PROTOCOLS (FIELD)

Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci) - ToLCV, ChiLCV vector:
- (INSP, KA) Yellow sticky traps: 1 trap/100 m² at crop canopy height [S1].
- (INSP, KA) Count adults/trap/week; >10 adults/trap/week = HIGH virus risk [S1].
- (INSP, KA) Leaf inspection: Check 5 leaves/plant, 10 plants/location for nymphs/adults on undersides [S1].
- (INSP, KA) Confirm vector species: Bemisia tabaci (MEAM1/Asia-II-1) - requires lab [S1].
- (INSP, KA) Virus confirmation: Symptomatic leaf samples to KVK/lab for PCR [S2].

Aphids (Aphis gossypii, Myzus persicae) - CMV, ChiVMV vector:
- (INSP, KA) Yellow sticky traps: winged aphid counts [S3].
- (INSP, KA) Direct inspection: Growing tips, young leaves for colonies [S3].
- (INSP, KA) >5 aphids/leaf on 10% plants = HIGH virus risk [S3].
- (INSP, KA) Virus confirmation: Symptomatic samples to KVK/lab for ELISA/PCR [S2].

Thrips (Scirtothrips dorsalis, Thrips palmi, Frankliniella schultzei) - TSWV/GBNV vector:
- (INSP, KA) Blue sticky traps: more attractive than yellow [S4].
- (INSP, KA) Tap flowers/leaves over white paper; count thrips [S4].
- (INSP, KA) >10 thrips/flower = HIGH virus risk [S4].
- (INSP, KA) Virus confirmation: Symptomatic samples to KVK/lab [S2].

WEATHER & SENSOR CONDITIONS FOR VECTOR ACTIVITY & TRANSMISSION

Whitefly:
- (FAV, KA) Temperature 25-30°C: optimal flight, reproduction [pest_whitefly].
- (FAV, KA) Humidity 60-75%: optimal survival [pest_whitefly].
- (FAV, KA) Low rainfall, high sunlight_wm2: high flight activity [pest_whitefly].
- (FAV, KA) Virus transmission efficiency: highest at 25-30°C [pest_whitefly].

Aphids:
- (FAV, KA) Temperature 20-25°C: optimal reproduction [pest_aphids_thrips].
- (FAV, KA) Humidity 70-80%: optimal survival [pest_aphids_thrips].
- (FAV, KA) Dry calm weather: winged dispersal [pest_aphids_thrips].
- (FAV, KA) Virus transmission: persistent/non-persistent depending on virus [S3].

Thrips:
- (FAV, KA) Temperature 25-30°C: optimal [pest_aphids_thrips].
- (FAV, KA) Humidity 40-60%: optimal for chilli thrips [pest_aphids_thrips].
- (FAV, KA) High sunlight, low leaf_wet: high activity [pest_aphids_thrips].
- (FAV, KA) Virus transmission: persistent (TSWV) [S4].

SEASONAL VIRUS RISK (KARNATAKA)

Rabi (Oct-Feb) - HIGHEST VIRUS RISK:
- Whitefly PEAK (Dec-Feb) → ToLCV, ChiLCV risk HIGH [pest_whitefly].
- Aphid PEAK (Jan-Feb) → CMV, ChiVMV risk HIGH [pest_aphids_thrips].
- Cool nights slow vectors but extend virus retention [S1][S3].

Summer (Feb-May):
- Thrips PEAK (Mar-May) → TSWV/GBNV risk HIGH [pest_aphids_thrips].
- Whitefly decline after March (heat >35°C) [pest_whitefly].
- Heat reduces virus replication in plant [S2].

Kharif (Jun-Oct):
- Moderate vector pressure; rains suppress [S1][S3][S4].
- Late kharif (Sep-Oct): vector build-up as rains recede [S1].

FIELD CONFIRMATION WORKFLOW

1. SENSOR ALERT: Conditions favour vector (see pest docs + this doc)
2. SCOUT: Deploy sticky traps (yellow for whitefly/aphid, blue for thrips)
3. INSPECT: Check leaves/flowers for vectors (nymphs, adults, colonies)
4. SYMPTOM CHECK: Look for virus symptoms (see 12, chilli_viral_diseases)
5. SAMPLE: Collect symptomatic leaves (with vectors if possible)
6. SUBMIT: Send to KVK/lab for PCR/ELISA confirmation
7. MANAGE: Based on confirmation - rogue, vector control, resistant varieties

DIFFERENTIATING VIRUS FROM LOOK-ALIKES

Whitefly feeding damage vs ToLCV/ChiLCV:
- Feeding: chlorotic spots, yellowing, downward curling; NO stunting, NO bushy [12].
- Virus: severe stunting, downward rolling, crinkling, leathery, bushy, short internodes [12].

Aphid feeding damage vs CMV/ChiVMV:
- Feeding: leaf curling, distortion, honeydew; NO mosaic [S3].
- Virus: mosaic (light/dark green), distortion, stunting, ringspots on fruit [chilli_viral_diseases].

Thrips feeding damage vs TSWV/GBNV:
- Feeding: silvery streaks, leaf curling, flower scarring [pest_aphids_thrips].
- Virus: bud necrosis, concentric ringspots, stunting, plant death [chilli_viral_diseases].

Physiological leaf curl vs viral:
- Physiological: upward curl, thick/leathery, green, NO stunting, NO vectors [tomato_nutrient_deficiencies][chilli_nutrient_deficiencies].
- Viral: downward curl, crinkling, stunting, bushy, vectors present [12][chilli_viral_diseases].

SENSOR READING INTERPRETATION FOR VIRUS RISK

HIGH virus transmission risk:
- Vector risk HIGH (see pest docs) + susceptible crop stage (seedling to early flowering) + susceptible variety + weed hosts present [S1][S3].

MODERATE virus transmission risk:
- Vector risk MODERATE + crop in susceptible stage [S1][S3].

LOW virus transmission risk:
- Vector risk LOW (heavy rain, temp extremes) OR crop past susceptible stage (late fruiting) [S1][S3].

CORPUS RULE: These are conditions that favour vector activity and virus transmission. They do not confirm virus infection. Lab confirmation required. KVK guides sampling and testing.
