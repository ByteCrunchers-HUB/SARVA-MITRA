TITLE: Other ragi (finger millet) diseases, resistant varieties and growing notes
CROPS: ragi (finger millet)
SENSOR_SIGNALS: weak; mostly crop-stage and variety information
EMBED: yes

IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, IN) Finger millet is susceptible to blast, sheath blight, seedling blight, foot rot, smut, leaf spot, green ear, damping-off, and streak and mottling viruses [S1].
- (OBS, IN) ICAR-IIMR: seedling diseases (root rot, damping-off) appear at the seedling stage. Cercospora and Helminthosporium leaf spots occur in finger millet and are sporadic [S2].
- (OBS, INTL) A book chapter lists crazy top, smut, downy mildew, foot rot, banded blight, sheath blight, bacterial leaf spot/blight/stripe, and mottle streak, severe mosaic and streak virus diseases, and states that blast is the most destructive [S3].

SYMPTOMS
- (INSP, SEC) Helminthosporium leaf spot: small oval brown lesions on young leaves that join into blighted patches [S4]. This is course slide material (secondary).

VARIETIES
- (OBS, IN) CFMV-1 (Indravathi), recommended for Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Odisha, is listed as resistant to finger blast, neck blast, banded blight and foot rot [S5].
- (OBS, IN) CFMV-2 is listed as resistant to leaf blast, foot rot, brown spot and grain mold, and moderately resistant to neck and finger blast and banded blight [S5].
- CORPUS NOTE: Variety suitability for a given farm should be confirmed with the KVK or UAS Bengaluru. The ICAR list is national.

GROWING NOTES
- (OBS, IN) About 90% of finger millet area is kharif rainfed. Ragi prefers loamy to sandy soil with good drainage [S5].
- (OBS, KA) Karnataka has the largest ragi area, with significant production in Bengaluru, Kolar, Tumakuru, Chitradurga and Hassan districts [S6].
- (WEAK, SEC) Seed treatment and foliar fungicides are mentioned for blast and leaf spot in course slides. Product names, doses and timing are not included here. Ask the KVK [S4].

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- UNAVAILABLE: No sensor threshold is available in this corpus for the ragi diseases listed here. For blast, see 15_ragi_blast and 16_ragi_weather_uas_bengaluru_tables.
- CORPUS RULE: Sensor readings cannot confirm any ragi disease named here.
