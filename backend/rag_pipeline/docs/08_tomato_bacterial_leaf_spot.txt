DOC_ID: 08_tomato_bacterial_leaf_spot
TITLE: Bacterial leaf spot of tomato and chilli - moist weather with splashing rain
CROPS: tomato; chilli
SENSOR_SIGNALS: rainfall_mm (splashing rain); humidity_pct; leaf_wet (persistent dew)
EMBED: yes

WEATHER FACTS
- (FAV, IN) Moist weather and splattering rain favour the disease on tomato, with high humidity or persistent dew [S1][S2].
- (FAV, IN) For chilli bacterial leaf spot, the NIPHM source lists moderate temperature, high relative humidity and intermittent rain as favourable [S3].

IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, IN) Bacteria survive on infected plant debris, on and inside seed, and on nightshade-family weeds such as Solanum nigrum. Secondary spread is by rain splash [S1].
- (OBS, IN) For chilli, primary infection is seed-borne and spread is by rain splash [S3].
