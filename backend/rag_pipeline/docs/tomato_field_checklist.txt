DOC_ID: tomato_field_checklist
TITLE: Tomato - what to look for today (field inspection checklist by sensor condition)
CROPS: tomato
SENSOR_SIGNALS: temperature_c; humidity_pct; leaf_wet; rainfall_mm; soil_moisture_pct; soil_ec_dsm; sunlight_wm2
EMBED: yes

CORPUS NOTE: This checklist helps the farmer know what to inspect in the field based on current sensor readings. It does not diagnose. Matching sensor conditions only favour a problem; they do not confirm it. Use with the relevant disease documents (03-12, 18-20, tomato_nutrient_deficiencies, tomato_blight_leafspot_comparison).

HOW TO USE
1. Look at your current sensor readings (especially consecutive readings over hours/days).
2. Find the matching condition row below.
3. Do the field checks listed for that condition.
4. Contact KVK or agriculture officer to confirm any findings.

CONDITION: Cool (10-21°C) + High humidity (>80%) + Long leaf wetness (>10 hrs)
→ PRIORITY CHECKS FOR LATE BLIGHT (03_late_blight_tomato_potato)
- Check leaf UNDERSIDES on humid mornings for WHITE MILDEW-LIKE GROWTH [03, S2]
- Check for water-soaked dark green patches turning purple-black [03, S2]
- Check stems/branches for brown lesions [03, S2]
- Check fruit for brown, firm, leathery lesions [03, S2]
→ ALSO CHECK FOR SEPTORIA (05_septoria_leaf_spot_tomato)
- Check LOWEST leaves for many small circular spots with dark margins, tan-grey centres, TINY BLACK DOTS [05, S2]
- Check for leaf yellowing/browning spreading upward [05, S2]

CONDITION: Warm (20-30°C) + High humidity/dew (>90%) + Alternating wet/dry periods
→ PRIORITY CHECKS FOR EARLY BLIGHT (04_early_blight_tomato_potato)
- Check OLDEST (lowest) leaves first for TARGET-LIKE SPOTS with concentric rings and yellow halo [04, S4]
- Check stems for dry, brown, ringed lesions [04, S2]
- Check fruit for concentric rings, often at stem end [04, S4]
→ ALSO CHECK FOR BACTERIAL LEAF SPOT (08_tomato_bacterial_leaf_spot)
- Check leaves for SMALL GREASY WATER-SOAKED SPOTS with YELLOW HALO (not vein-limited) [08, S4]
- Check green fruit for SUNKEN SCABBY SPOTS with yellow halo [08, S4]

CONDITION: Moderate (20-25°C) + High humidity + Poor airflow + Lower leaves affected
→ PRIORITY CHECKS FOR SEPTORIA (05_septoria_leaf_spot_tomato)
- Check LOWEST leaves for MANY SMALL CIRCULAR SPOTS with dark margins, tan-grey centres, TINY BLACK DOTS [05, S2]
- Check for leaf yellowing, browning, withering spreading upward [05, S2]
→ ALSO CHECK FOR EARLY BLIGHT (04_early_blight_tomato_potato)
- Check oldest leaves for LARGER target-like spots with concentric rings [04, S4]

CONDITION: High humidity + Moderate temp (16-25°C) + Dry leaves (leaf_wet=0) + Shade
→ CHECK FOR POWDERY MILDEW (06_powdery_mildew_tomato_chilli)
- Turn leaves over; check LOWER SURFACE for WHITE POWDERY GROWTH [06, S5]
- Check UPPER surface for PALE YELLOW BLOTCHES [06, S5]

CONDITION: High humidity (>80%) + 15.5-24°C + Dense canopy
→ CHECK FOR GRAY MOLD (07_gray_mold_botrytis_tomato)
- Check wounds, dead tissue, fading flowers for GREY-BROWN FUZZY GROWTH [07, S2]
- Check fruit for soft rot and "ghost spots" [07, S4]

CONDITION: Rainfall/splashing + High humidity + Persistent dew
→ CHECK FOR BACTERIAL LEAF SPOT (08_tomato_bacterial_leaf_spot)
- Check leaves for small greasy spots with YELLOW HALO (not vein-limited) [08, S4]
- Check green fruit for raised water-soaked spots with yellow border [08, S4]

CONDITION: High soil moisture (days) + High temperature (29-35°C)
→ CHECK FOR BACTERIAL WILT (09_bacterial_wilt_ralstonia)
- Check for SUDDEN COMPLETE WILTING of normal-looking green plants [09, S8]
- Check stem base for brown discoloration [09, S2]

CONDITION: Low soil moisture + Light sandy soil + 27-32°C
→ CHECK FOR FUSARIUM WILT (10_fusarium_wilt_tomato)
- Check for ONE-SIDED YELLOWING on lower leaves [10, S2]
- Check stem base for BROWN STREAKING inside [10, S2]

CONDITION: Wet, cool, poorly drained soil (seedlings)
→ CHECK FOR DAMPING-OFF (11_damping_off_seedlings)
- Check stem at soil line for WATER-SOAKED TISSUE, collapse [11, S5]

CONDITION: Curling leaves + Whiteflies present
→ CHECK FOR LEAF CURL VIRUS (12_tomato_leaf_curl_virus_whitefly)
- Check for SEVERE STUNTING, downward rolling, crinkling, leathery leaves, bushy look [12, S1]
- Check for WHITEFLIES on undersides [12, S1]

CONDITION: Swinging soil moisture (dry-wet cycles) + Heat + High soil EC
→ CHECK FOR BLOSSOM-END ROT (18_blossom_end_rot_tomato)
- Check FRUIT blossom end (away from stem) for TAN WATER-SOAKED area turning BLACK LEATHERY [18, S3]

CONDITION: High temperature (>29°C day / >21°C night) + Flower drop
→ CHECK FOR HEAT STRESS (19_heat_stress_fruit_set_tomato)
- Check for FLOWER/BUD DROP and poor fruit set [19, S4]

CONDITION: Rising soil_ec_dsm (>2.5 dS/m trend) + Salt-sensitive variety
→ CHECK FOR SALT STRESS (20_soil_salinity_ec_tomato)
- Check for reduced leaf area, chlorophyll loss, yield decline [20, S4]

CONDITION: Young leaves: pale margins, necrotic tips, dead growing point
→ CHECK FOR CALCIUM or BORON DEFICIENCY (tomato_nutrient_deficiencies)
- Check fruit for BLOSSOM-END ROT [tomato_nutrient_deficiencies, S1]

CONDITION: Older leaves: interveinal yellowing (veins green)
→ CHECK FOR MAGNESIUM DEFICIENCY (tomato_nutrient_deficiencies)

CONDITION: Older leaves: edge burning, curling, crinkling
→ CHECK FOR POTASSIUM DEFICIENCY (tomato_nutrient_deficiencies)

CONDITION: Older leaves: uniform yellowing from tips
→ CHECK FOR NITROGEN DEFICIENCY (tomato_nutrient_deficiencies)

CONDITION: Lower leaves: upward curl, thick leathery, green
→ CHECK FOR PHYSIOLOGICAL LEAF CURL (tomato_nutrient_deficiencies)
- Distinct from viral: no stunting, no crinkling, no whiteflies [tomato_nutrient_deficiencies, S1]

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- All thresholds come from documents cited above, primarily INTL (USA) or IN (TNAU, NIPHM). No Karnataka-measured thresholds for tomato foliar diseases.
- CORPUS RULE: This checklist only prioritizes field inspection. Sensor readings show conditions that favour a problem. They do not confirm it. Field inspection and KVK/agriculture officer confirmation are required.
