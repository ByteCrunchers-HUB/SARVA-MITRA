TITLE: Ragi (finger millet) crop-stage sensor expectations and management focus - Karnataka
CROPS: ragi (finger millet)
SENSOR_SIGNALS: temperature_c; humidity_pct; rainfall_mm; soil_moisture_pct; sunlight_wm2; leaf_wet
EMBED: yes

CORPUS NOTE: Ragi is predominantly kharif rainfed (90%) in Karnataka. This document defines expected sensor ranges and management priorities for each crop stage. Ranges are approximate (Karnataka UAS/UAS GKVK/AICRP guidance); actual values vary by zone, soil, and monsoon. Sensors show conditions, not diagnoses.

CORPUS RULE: No universal sensor thresholds. Ragi is rainfed; irrigation rare. Use as general guidance. KVK consultation for farm-specific targets.

IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, KA) Ragi grown in kharif (Jun-Nov) in Karnataka; 120-day medium duration [S1].
- (OBS, KA) Major zones: Zone 5 (Bengaluru, Eastern Dry), Zone 6 (Mysore, Southern Dry) [S2].
- (OBS, KA) UAS Bengaluru Package of Practices, UAS GKVK, AICRP Agrometeorology define stages [S1][S2][S3].

STAGE 1: SOWING TO GERMINATION (0-15 DAS)
- Timing: Zone 5/6 - Sowing window 1 (early Jun), window 2 (late Jun) [S2].
- Temperature_c: Optimum 25-30°C for germination [S1].
- Rainfall_mm: 20-30 mm for sowing moisture; avoid dry sowing [S1].
- Soil_moisture_pct: >16% volumetric (red sandy loam) for germination [S4][ragi_soil_moisture_ka].
- Sunlight_wm2: Full sun after emergence [S1].
- Leaf_wet: Not applicable (bare soil) [S1].
- Key risks: Dry sowing failure, seedling damping-off (11) if waterlogged [S5].
- Management focus: Wait for monsoon onset, seed treatment, proper depth (3-5 cm), drainage.

STAGE 2: SEEDLING / ESTABLISHMENT (15-30 DAS)
- Duration: 2-4 weeks after emergence
- Temperature_c: 25-30°C day / 18-22°C night [S1].
- Humidity_pct: 70-85% (monsoon) [S1].
- Rainfall_mm: Regular monsoon rain expected [S1].
- Soil_moisture_pct: >16% volumetric (adequate) [S4].
- Sunlight_wm2: Increasing as canopy develops [S1].
- Key risks: Damping-off (11) if waterlogged, blast (15) early infection possible [S5][S6].
- Management focus: Gap filling, weed management (critical 20-30 DAS), drainage.

STAGE 3: VEGETATIVE / TILLERING (30-60 DAS)
- Duration: Peak tillering at 40-50 DAS
- Temperature_c: 25-28°C day / 20-22°C night [S1].
- Humidity_pct: 75-90% (monsoon peak) [S1].
- Rainfall_mm: Regular to heavy [S1].
- Soil_moisture_pct: >18% volumetric (adequate monsoon) [S4].
- Sunlight_wm2: Variable; cloudy days common [S1].
- Key risks: Blast (15, ragi_blast_detailed) - leaf blast starts [S6], stem borer (AICRP: 25-29°C, humidity 83-95%) [S2], leaf folder (25-29°C, humidity >80%) [S2].
- Management focus: Weed management, blast scouting (lower leaves), stem borer monitoring.

STAGE 4: FLOWERING (60-80 DAS) - CRITICAL FOR YIELD
- Duration: 50% flowering at ~70 DAS
- Temperature_c: 24-27°C day / 18-21°C night [S1].
- Humidity_pct: 80-95% morning; critical for blast [S2][S6].
- Rainfall_mm: Frequent rain/drizzle favours blast [S6].
- Soil_moisture_pct: Target >16% volumetric (58% PASM) [S4][ragi_soil_moisture_ka].
- Sunlight_wm2: Cloudy days reduce photosynthesis [S1].
- Key risks: Blast (15, ragi_blast_detailed) - NECK BLAST most damaging [S6], grain mold if humid at maturity [S7].
- Management focus: Blast scouting (neck & fingers), protective irrigation if dry spell >10 days, avoid excess N.

STAGE 5: GRAIN FILLING (80-105 DAS) - MOST CRITICAL
- Duration: 25 days; grain weight accumulation
- Temperature_c: 24-28°C day / 18-22°C night [S1].
- Humidity_pct: 75-90% morning [S1].
- Rainfall_mm: Decreasing; dry spells = moisture stress risk [S1].
- Soil_moisture_pct: CRITICAL - maintain >16% volumetric (58% PASM) [S4][ragi_soil_moisture_ka].
- Sunlight_wm2: Full sun needed for grain fill [S1].
- Key risks: Moisture stress (if soil_moisture_pct <13% = 40% PASM = >75% yield loss) [S4], finger blast (ragi_blast_detailed), grain mold if humid [S7].
- Management focus: Moisture monitoring, supplemental irrigation if available, blast scouting (fingers).

STAGE 6: MATURITY TO HARVEST (105-120 DAS)
- Duration: Physiological maturity at ~110 DAS
- Temperature_c: 22-28°C day / 16-20°C night [S1].
- Humidity_pct: 60-75% (decreasing) [S1].
- Rainfall_mm: Dry weather NEEDED [S1].
- Soil_moisture_pct: <14% volumetric at harvest [S1].
- Sunlight_wm2: Full sun for drying [S1].
- Key risks: Grain mold (17) if rain at maturity, shattering if over-mature [S1][S7].
- Management focus: Timely harvest, proper drying, storage.

ZONE-SPECIFIC NOTES (UAS BENGALURU AICRP)

Zone 5 (Bengaluru District - Eastern Dry Zone):
- (OBS, KA) Red sandy loam; weekly minimum temp 15-19.6°C [S2].
- (FAV, KA) Neck+finger blast incidence 4.8-96.25% across weeks [S2].
- (FAV, KA) Leaf folder favoured at 25-29°C, humidity >80% [S2].
- (FAV, KA) Stem borer at 25-29°C, humidity 83-95% [S2].

Zone 6 (Mysore District - Southern Dry Zone):
- (OBS, KA) Red sandy loam; morning humidity 90-94%, >3 cloudy days [S2].
- (FAV, KA) Blast incidence 8-45%; neck+finger blast 5-96% [S2].
- (FAV, KA) Stem borer at 25-29°C, humidity 83-95% [S2].

North Karnataka (Black Soils - Bidar, Kalaburagi, Vijayapura):
- (OBS, KA) Later sowing (Jul-Aug); longer duration varieties [S1].
- UNAVAILABLE: Zone-specific sensor thresholds for black soils.

SENSOR MONITORING PRIORITIES BY STAGE

| Stage | Primary Sensors | Watch Thresholds (Red Sandy Loam) |
|-------|----------------|-----------------------------------|
| Sowing | rainfall_mm, soil_moisture_pct, temperature_c | rain <20mm, soil <16%, temp <20°C |
| Seedling | soil_moisture_pct, rainfall_mm, humidity_pct | soil <14% or >22%, heavy rain |
| Tillering | humidity_pct, rainfall_mm, temperature_c | humidity >90%+cloudy, temp 25-29°C |
| Flowering | humidity_pct (morning), rainfall_mm, soil_moisture_pct | humidity >90%+rain, soil <16% |
| Grain Fill | soil_moisture_pct, rainfall_mm, humidity_pct | soil <13% (SEVERE), dry spell >10 days |
| Harvest | rainfall_mm, humidity_pct, soil_moisture_pct | rain >0, humidity >75%, soil >14% |

CORPUS RULE: These are approximate Karnataka red sandy loam ranges. Actual values vary by zone, soil, and monsoon. Sensors show conditions, not diagnoses. Field inspection and KVK consultation required.

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- (OBS, KA) UAS Package of Practices, AICRP Agrometeorology tables, UAS GKVK study provide Karnataka data.
- UNAVAILABLE: Continuous sensor validation for all stages across all Karnataka zones.
- UNAVAILABLE: Sensor thresholds for black soils, laterite soils.
