TITLE: Chilli - what to look for today (field inspection checklist by sensor condition)
CROPS: chilli
SENSOR_SIGNALS: temperature_c; humidity_pct; leaf_wet; rainfall_mm; soil_moisture_pct; soil_ec_dsm; sunlight_wm2
EMBED: yes

CORPUS NOTE: This checklist helps the farmer know what to inspect in the field based on current sensor readings. It does not diagnose. Matching sensor conditions only favour a problem; they do not confirm it. Use with the relevant disease documents (06, 08, 13, 14, chilli_bacterial_leaf_spot, chilli_wilt_damping_off, chilli_heat_stress, chilli_viral_diseases, chilli_nutrient_deficiencies).

HOW TO USE
1. Look at your current sensor readings (especially consecutive readings over hours/days).
2. Find the matching condition row below.
3. Do the field checks listed for that condition.
4. Contact KVK or agriculture officer to confirm any findings.

CONDITION: High humidity (>95%) + Temperature ~28°C + Frequent rain (especially as fruit ripens)
→ PRIORITY CHECKS FOR ANTHRACNOSE (13_chilli_anthracnose_dieback_fruit_rot)
- Check RIPENING FRUIT for BLACK CIRCULAR SPOTS (bird's eye spots) [13, S1]
- Check badly affected fruit for STRAW-COLOURED/WHITE discoloration, loss of pungency [13, S1]
- Check branch tips for DIEBACK [13, S1]
- Check leaves for brownish-black spots [13, S1]

CONDITION: Moderate temperature + High humidity + Intermittent rain
→ CHECK FOR BACTERIAL LEAF SPOT (chilli_bacterial_leaf_spot)
- Check leaves for SMALL GREASY DARK SPOTS with DARK BAND around lighter centres [chilli_bacterial_leaf_spot, S4]
- Check FRUIT for ROUND RAISED WATER-SOAKED SPOTS with PALE YELLOW BORDER (key diagnostic) [chilli_bacterial_leaf_spot, S4]
- Check for SHINY BACTERIAL OOZE in centre of older fruit spots [chilli_bacterial_leaf_spot, S4]

CONDITION: Cool dry weather for spore germination + High humidity for development
→ CHECK FOR POWDERY MILDEW (06_powdery_mildew_tomato_chilli)
- Turn leaves over; check LOWER SURFACE for WHITE POWDERY GROWTH [06, S7]
- Check UPPER surface for PALE YELLOW BLOTCHES [06, S7]

CONDITION: Warm, rainy, wet weather
→ CHECK FOR CERCOSPORA / ALTERNARIA LEAF SPOT (14_chilli_other_diseases_conditions)
- Check leaves for circular brown spots (Cercospora) or target-like spots (Alternaria) [14, S1]
- UNAVAILABLE: Detailed symptom descriptions not in corpus

CONDITION: High soil moisture (days) + Temperature 25-30°C + Heavy rain / poor drainage
→ CHECK FOR PHYTOPHTHORA / PYTHIUM WILT & DAMPING-OFF (chilli_wilt_damping_off)
- Check stem base/crown for WATER-SOAKED DARK GREEN/BLACK LESIONS (Phytophthora) [chilli_wilt_damping_off, S1]
- Check for SUDDEN WILTING of green plants, rapid death [chilli_wilt_damping_off, S1]
- Check NURSERY beds: collapsed seedlings, water-soaked collar (Pythium) [chilli_wilt_damping_off, S2]

CONDITION: High soil moisture + Temperature 30-35°C
→ CHECK FOR RHIZOCTONIA ROOT/STEM ROT (chilli_wilt_damping_off)
- Check stem at soil line for REDDISH-BROWN DRY SUNKEN LESIONS ("wirestem") [chilli_wilt_damping_off, S3]
- Check for stunting, lower leaf yellowing, plant collapse [chilli_wilt_damping_off, S3]

CONDITION: High soil moisture + Warm soil temperature (NIPHM)
→ CHECK FOR CHILLI FUSARIUM WILT (14_chilli_other_diseases_conditions)
- CORPUS NOTE: This differs from tomato Fusarium (low soil moisture). Difference not resolved.
- Check for wilting, yellowing, stem base discoloration [14, S1]

CONDITION: High temperature (>32°C day / >24°C night) + Low humidity (<50%) + Flower drop
→ CHECK FOR HEAT STRESS (chilli_heat_stress)
- Check for FLOWER/BUD DROP and poor fruit set [chilli_heat_stress, S2]
- Check for SMALL MISSHAPEN/PARTHENOCARPIC FRUIT [chilli_heat_stress, S2]
- Check leaves for UPWARD CURLING (physiological leaf curl), thick, leathery [chilli_heat_stress, S4]

CONDITION: High humidity (>80%) prolonged + High temperature
→ CHECK FOR HUMIDITY-HINDERED POLLEN SHEDDING (chilli_heat_stress)
- Check for poor fruit set despite flowers [chilli_heat_stress, S3]

CONDITION: Mosaic pattern on leaves + Aphids present
→ CHECK FOR CHILLI MOSAIC / CMV (chilli_viral_diseases)
- Check for LIGHT/DARK GREEN MOSAIC, leaf distortion, stunting [chilli_viral_diseases, S1]
- Check for APHIDS on growing tips [chilli_viral_diseases, S1]

CONDITION: Severe stunting + Downward leaf rolling/crinkling + Leathery leaves + Whiteflies
→ CHECK FOR CHILLI LEAF CURL VIRUS (chilli_viral_diseases)
- Check for BUSHY LOOK with more side branches [chilli_viral_diseases, S2]
- Check for WHITEFLIES on undersides [chilli_viral_diseases, S2]
- CORPUS NOTE: Whitefly feeding alone can curl leaves without virus [chilli_viral_diseases, S5]

CONDITION: Vein clearing, mottling, distortion + Aphids
→ CHECK FOR CHILLI VEINAL MOTTLE VIRUS (chilli_viral_diseases)

CONDITION: Bud necrosis + Ringspots on leaves/fruit + Thrips in flowers
→ CHECK FOR GBNV / TSWV (chilli_viral_diseases)
- Check for NECROSIS OF GROWING BUDS [chilli_viral_diseases, S3]
- Check for CONCENTRIC RINGSPOTS [chilli_viral_diseases, S3]
- Check for THRIPS in flowers [chilli_viral_diseases, S3]

CONDITION: Swinging soil moisture + Heat + High soil EC
→ CHECK FOR BLOSSOM-END ROT (physiological, like tomato 18)
- Check FRUIT blossom end for SUNKEN LEATHERY SPOT [tomato 18, S3; chilli_nutrient_deficiencies, S4]

CONDITION: Young leaves: distorted/hooked, tip burn, dead growing point
→ CHECK FOR CALCIUM or BORON DEFICIENCY (chilli_nutrient_deficiencies)
- Check fruit for BLOSSOM-END ROT [chilli_nutrient_deficiencies, S2]

CONDITION: Older leaves: interveinal yellowing (veins green)
→ CHECK FOR MAGNESIUM DEFICIENCY (chilli_nutrient_deficiencies)

CONDITION: Older leaves: edge burning, curling
→ CHECK FOR POTASSIUM DEFICIENCY (chilli_nutrient_deficiencies)
- Check fruit for POOR SIZE, UNEVEN COLOR, LOW PUNGENCY [chilli_nutrient_deficiencies, S3]

CONDITION: Older leaves: uniform yellowing from tips
→ CHECK FOR NITROGEN DEFICIENCY (chilli_nutrient_deficiencies)

CONDITION: Older leaves: purplish undersides
→ CHECK FOR PHOSPHORUS DEFICIENCY (chilli_nutrient_deficiencies)

CONDITION: Leaves: upward curl, thick leathery, green (no stunting, no vectors)
→ CHECK FOR PHYSIOLOGICAL LEAF CURL (chilli_nutrient_deficiencies)
- Distinct from viral: no mosaic, no stunting, no bushy look, no vectors [chilli_nutrient_deficiencies, S6]

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- Most thresholds come from IN (TNAU, NIPHM) sources; no Karnataka-measured thresholds for chilli foliar diseases.
- Heat stress values from INTL/IN (Punjab, TNAU), not Karnataka-specific.
- Soil moisture values from NIPHM use undefined units.
- CORPUS RULE: This checklist only prioritizes field inspection. Sensor readings show conditions that favour a problem. They do not confirm it. Field inspection and KVK/agriculture officer confirmation are required.
