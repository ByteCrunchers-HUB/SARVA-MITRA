DOC_ID: 05_septoria_leaf_spot_tomato
TITLE: Septoria leaf spot (Septoria lycopersici) - conditions that favour it
CROPS: tomato
SENSOR_SIGNALS: temperature_c; humidity_pct; leaf_wet; rainfall_mm (splash); canopy airflow (farmer-reported)
EMBED: yes

IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, INTL) Septoria leaf spot is caused by Septoria lycopersici and affects tomato and other nightshade-family plants [S1][S3].
- (OBS, INTL) It spreads by splashing rain, equipment, workers or insects, survives on tomato debris and solanaceous weeds, and can be seed-borne [S3].

WEATHER FACTS
- (FAV, INTL, USA) High humidity, moderate temperature of 20-25°C, high dew point or wet conditions, and poor airflow favour the disease [S1].
- (FAV, INTL, USA) Long high humidity with 15-27°C and leaf wetness are described as ideal [S2].
- (FAV, INTL, USA) Heat waves with long stretches of high temperature and humidity, or repeated periods of wet foliage such as long morning dew, favour it [S3].
- (WEAK, INTL) A growth-chamber study found most lesions at 20-25°C, though lesions still formed at 10°C. With humidity of 50-60% and no dew, lesion numbers were similar to 16 hours of dew on inoculated leaves, so some disease can occur even under unfavourable weather [S4].

SYMPTOMS
- (INSP, INTL) Many small circular spots on the lowest, oldest leaves, with dark margins and tan to grey centres with tiny black dots. It spreads upward; leaves yellow, brown and wither [S2].
- (INSP, INTL) Fruit infection is rare [S2]; the source says it does not directly affect fruit [S5].

FIELD INSPECTION
- (INSP, INTL) Check the lowest, oldest leaves first for small spots with dark margins [S2].

MANAGEMENT
- (OBS, INTL) Remove lower growth, keep plant spacing, control host weeds, destroy debris and avoid over-irrigating [S1].
- (OBS, INTL) Water at the base. Prune the lowest 3-4 leaf branches once plants are established [S6].
- (OBS, INTL) If caught early, remove infected lower leaves. Removing leaves above fruit weakens the plant and exposes fruit to sunscald [S2].
- (OBS, INTL) The disease can be harder to control than early blight [S3]. The source names preventive fungicide use; product names, doses and timing are not included here. Ask the KVK.

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- UNAVAILABLE: No Karnataka or ICAR Septoria threshold is present in this corpus.
- CORPUS RULE: Matching sensor readings show that conditions favour Septoria leaf spot. They do not confirm it.
