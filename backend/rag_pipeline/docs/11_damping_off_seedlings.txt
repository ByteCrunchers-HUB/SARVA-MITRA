TITLE: Damping-off of seedlings and nursery plants - conditions that favour it
CROPS: tomato; chilli; ragi (finger millet)
SENSOR_SIGNALS: soil_moisture_pct (very high); temperature_c (cool or warm soil); sunlight_wm2 (low light, cloudy days); rainfall_mm; irrigation practice (farmer-reported)
EMBED: yes

IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, INTL) Damping-off is caused by several soil organisms: Pythium, Phytophthora, Rhizoctonia and Fusarium [S1][S2].

SOIL AND WEATHER FACTS
- (FAV, INTL, USA) Pythium and Phytophthora favour cool, wet, poorly drained soil. Fusarium and Rhizoctonia are more common under warm, dry conditions. Cool wet soil slows seedling growth, which lengthens the susceptible period [S3]. Rhizoctonia can also cause disease in dry soil [S4].
- (FAV, INTL, USA) Low light, overwatering, high salts from over-fertilising and cool soil are linked to more damping-off [S5].
- (FAV, INTL, USA) If cloudy, cool weather keeps trays wet for days, Pythium is likely; Pythium also shows in low spots where plants sit in water [S2].
- (FAV, INTL, Australia) Pythium and Rhizoctonia are common in both tropical and temperate soils and thrive in wet soil, especially when temperature suits the pathogen more than the host [S6].
- (FAV, IN) For chilli, NIPHM lists heavy rainfall, excessive and frequent irrigation, poorly drained soil, close spacing and high soil moisture with about 25-30°C for Pythium, and about 30-35°C for Rhizoctonia [S7].

SYMPTOMS
- (INSP, INTL) Seeds rot or seedlings fail to emerge; stems at or below the soil line become water-soaked and collapse; seedlings fall over [S5][S8].

FIELD INSPECTION
- (INSP, INTL) Check the stem at the soil line of fallen seedlings for water-soaked tissue [S5][S8].

MANAGEMENT AND PREVENTION
- (OBS, INTL) Seedlings that are killed cannot be saved; prevention is the only way [S8]. Established plants resist better [S9].
- (OBS, INTL) Do not overwater; water in late morning when soil is dry; use well-drained soil or raised beds; do not plant into cool soil; do not sow too densely or too deep [S3][S4][S8].
- (OBS, INTL) Use clean, sterilised trays and clean potting mix. Treated seed can reduce incidence; seed-treatment products and doses are not included here, ask the KVK [S5][S10].

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- UNAVAILABLE: No ragi-specific damping-off conditions were extracted for this document. Crop applicability to ragi is listed in 17_ragi_other_diseases_and_varieties, which names damping-off as a ragi disease [S11].
- CORPUS RULE: Wet, cool or cloudy readings show conditions that favour damping-off. They do not confirm it.
