TITLE: Chilli anthracnose, dieback and fruit rot (Colletotrichum) - conditions that favour it
CROPS: chilli
SENSOR_SIGNALS: temperature_c; humidity_pct; rainfall_mm (frequent rain, especially as fruit ripens)
EMBED: yes

IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, IN) Spread is by seed, airborne spores and rain. Flies and other insects carry spores between fruits [S1].
- (OBS, IN) NIPHM lists intercropping with turmeric (another host) as a favouring factor [S2].

WEATHER FACTS
- (FAV, IN) TNAU: favourable temperature 28°C and humidity 95%, and high humidity when rain occurs after fruits start to ripen [S1].
- (FAV, IN) NIPHM: temperature 28°C with humidity above 97%, and humid weather with frequent rainfall [S2].
- (FAV, IN, Haryana) A CCSHAU study found disease development was highest at 30°C and 100% humidity in the laboratory. In the field, maximum temperature 30.6-35.9°C, minimum 21.7-26.5°C, morning humidity 84.3-97% and evening humidity 54.3-58.3% suited the disease. Minimum and maximum temperature together explained 85% of severity [S3].
- (WEAK, IN) The thresholds above are from Tamil Nadu and Haryana sources and a thesis. Karnataka-measured values are not available in this corpus [S1][S3].

SYMPTOMS
- (INSP, IN) Black circular spots on ripening fruit. Badly affected fruit turn straw coloured or white and lose pungency. Brownish-black spots on leaves and dieback of branches also occur [S1][S2].

FIELD INSPECTION
- (INSP, IN) Check ripening fruit for black circular spots, and branch tips for dieback [S1][S2].

MANAGEMENT
- (OBS, IN) Use disease-free seed [S1].
- (WEAK, IN) TNAU and NIPHM mention chemical seed treatment and fungicide sprays. Product names, doses and spray timing are not included here. Ask the KVK or agriculture officer for approved products [S1][S4].
- (WEAK, IN) Biocontrol agents (Pseudomonas fluorescens and Bacillus subtilis) were reported as effective in studies [S1]. This is study evidence only, not a product recommendation.

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- CORPUS RULE: Matching readings show conditions that favour anthracnose. They do not confirm it.
