TITLE: Fusarium wilt of tomato (Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. lycopersici) - conditions that favour it
CROPS: tomato
SENSOR_SIGNALS: temperature_c; soil_moisture_pct (low or dry); humidity_pct; soil type and pH (farmer-reported)
EMBED: yes

IDENTIFICATION
- (OBS, INTL) Fusarium wilt is caused by Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. lycopersici. The fungus survives in soil or debris for up to ten years (TNAU and INRAE say more than ten) [S1][S3][S4].
- (OBS, IN, INTL) It spreads by seed, seedlings raised in infested soil, wind-blown soil, surface water and implements [S1][S2].

WEATHER AND SOIL FACTS
- (FAV, IN) TNAU lists alternating low and high soil temperature with high humidity, optimum temperature 28°C, light sandy soil, low soil moisture and low pH [S1].
- (FAV, INTL, Australia) The South Australian department names warm temperature around 28°C, dry weather and acidic soil. Fast-growing plants fertilised with ammonium nitrate are especially prone, and root-knot nematodes raise the chance of infection even in resistant plants [S2].
- (FAV, INTL, France) INRAE gives about 28°C and sandy acidic soils, and says nematode attack or oxygen-poor soil can sensitise plants [S3].
- (FAV, INTL, USA) Symptoms are most common at 27-32°C. The disease develops faster with high nitrogen and low potassium, and in sandy soils [S4].
- CORPUS NOTE: Several sources do not say whether the temperature is air or soil. The corpus sensor temperature_c is air temperature (see 01_dataset_columns_reference), so a match is only a rough signal.
- (OBS, IN, INTL) Sources link Fusarium wilt to low soil moisture and dry weather [S1][S2]. Bacterial wilt (see 09_bacterial_wilt_ralstonia) is instead linked to high soil moisture. Both cause wilting, so soil moisture alone cannot separate them.

SYMPTOMS
- (INSP, INTL, Australia, USA) Yellowing starting on lower leaves, often on one side of the plant or leaf, then leaf curling, browning and drying; brown streaking inside the stem base; stunting [S2][S4].

FIELD INSPECTION
- (INSP, INTL) Look for one-sided yellowing on lower leaves and brown streaking inside the stem base [S2][S4].

MANAGEMENT
- (OBS, IN) Remove and destroy infected plant debris [S1].
- (OBS, INTL) Clean structures, crates, tools and stakes. Stop soil moving between fields on equipment and shoes. Avoid working in wet plantings [S2][S4].
- (OBS, INTL) Avoid too much nitrogen with low potassium, and test the soil [S4].
- (OBS, IN) TNAU mentions seed treatment with a chemical. Product names, doses and timing are not included here. Ask the KVK [S1].

EVIDENCE QUALITY AND LIMITS
- UNAVAILABLE: No Karnataka-measured threshold is present in this corpus. TNAU is a Tamil Nadu source.
- CORPUS RULE: Matching readings show conditions that favour Fusarium wilt. They do not confirm it.
