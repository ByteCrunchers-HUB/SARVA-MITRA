Income Support Central Govt
🌱

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year transferred in 3 equal four-monthly installments into the Aadhaar-linked bank accounts of landholder farmer families.

Eligibility: All small, marginal, and landholding farmer families across India.
Visit PM-KISAN Portal
Soil Health DA&FW
🚜

Soil Health Card Scheme

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Provides farmers with tailored soil test reports for 12 essential parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) with crop-wise nutrient management guidance.

Benefits: Reduces excessive fertilizer expenses by up to 20% and prevents soil degradation.
Access Soil Health Portal
Crop Insurance Subsidized
🌾

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive insurance shield covering crop failure from pre-sowing to post-harvest against unseasonal rainfall, drought, floods, and localized pathogen outbreaks.

Farmer Premium: Only 2% for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi, and 5% for annual commercial crops.
Check Coverage & Premium
Advisory & Labs ICAR
🏛️

Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)

Agricultural Extension & Diagnostic Network

District-level agricultural science stations that provide on-field testing, certified disease diagnosis, seed verification, and free advisory by agronomists.

Locate: Find your nearest district KVK for certified disease sample testing.
Locate Nearest KVK
Organic Farming Assistance
🌿

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas

PKVY — Organic Farming Promotion

Financial assistance of ₹50,000 per hectare for 3 years to adopt organic inputs, bio-fertilizers, vermicompost, and cluster-based certification.

Goal: Sustainable chemical-free farming and higher market premiums for organic produce.
View Organic Guidelines
Credit & Loans Low Interest
💳

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Institutional Farm Credit Scheme

Affordable agricultural credit at a subsidized effective interest rate of 4% per annum for timely purchasing of seeds, fertilizers, irrigation electricity, and tools.

Limit: Up to ₹3 Lakh collateral-free borrowing for cultivation expenses.
